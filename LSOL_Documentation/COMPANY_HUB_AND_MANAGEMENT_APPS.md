# Company Hub And Management Apps

LSOL has two core management surfaces:

- `F7` for the Mod Control menu
- `F8` for the Company Hub tablet

If you keep that split in mind, the interface becomes much easier to navigate.

## Use `F7` For Global Management

`F7` is where you handle systems that sit outside the world markers.

That usually includes:

- Saving Options
- difficulty settings
- general mod options

If you want to create, load, delete, or manually save a profile, start here.

## Use `F8` For Company Intelligence

`F8` opens the Company Hub from anywhere.

This is the best place to review the current state of the company without driving across the map just to check one number.

![image](../doc_images/f8.JPG)

## Core Company Hub Pages

The Company Hub currently exposes these major surfaces:

- overview or home-style summary tiles
- Industries
- Stores
- Gas Stations
- Warehouses
- Dispatch
- Budget
- Analytics
- Successes
- Route Planner
- Company Map

## What Each Page Is Good For

### Overview

Use it for a quick company snapshot such as balance, fleet posture, property count, and general activity.

### Industries, Stores, Gas Stations, And Warehouses

Use these network pages to inspect tracked locations across the region.

They are the fastest way to answer questions like:

- which sites are healthy
- which sinks are undersupplied
- where storage risk is building
- which locations are worth visiting next

![image](../doc_images/f8_indus.JPG)

### Dispatch

Dispatch is the network-control layer for side contracts and ambient freight support.

Use it to:

- browse permit-free Quick Jobs and Freight Market contracts
- review accepted side contracts
- open the `World Dispatch` page
- bias ambient freight by policy, commodity, or district
- toggle premium dispatch
- inspect the `Dispatch Diagnostics` feed when ambient activity looks wrong or too quiet

### Budget

Budget is where LSOL's late-game economic pressure becomes readable.

It shows things like:

- weekly net position
- upcoming bills
- corporate overhead
- fleet maintenance pressure
- route profitability views
- inventory and warehouse-loss exposure

![image](../doc_images/f8_budget.JPG)

### Analytics

Analytics is for trends and comparisons rather than immediate action.

Use it to inspect:

- market movement
- commodity history
- route performance
- district pressure and competition visibility
- longer-running business patterns

![image](../doc_images/f8_analytics.JPG)

### Successes

Successes tracks milestone progress and your late-game identity layer. It becomes more important once doctrine, prestige, and HQ development enter the save.
