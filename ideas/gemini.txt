Geographic Zoning (Map Integration)To make the mod feel immersive, you should map specific industries to logical locations in San Andreas:Extraction (North): Place Ore and Coal Mines in the San Chianski Mountain Range or Great Chaparral. Oil Wells belong in the Grand Senora Desert or the El Burro Heights oil fields.Heavy Processing (Industrial South): The Smelting Factory and Oil Refinery should be located in the Cypress Flats or Terminal areas (the industrial ports).High-Tech Manufacturing (City/Suburbs): The Processor and Consumer Electronics Factories fit well in the warehouses near Los Santos International Airport (LSIA) or the Richards Majestic movie lot area.Recycling (Urban Hubs): Place Recycling Centers near the Scrapyard in La Puerta.2. Dynamic Logistics & Vehicle TiersIn GTA, the "gameplay" is the driving. You should create a progression system for transport:Resource TypeRecommended VehicleLogicRaw Ore/CoalTipper or Rubble (Dump Trucks)Slow, heavy, and difficult to maneuver off-road.Crude OilPhantom/Hauler with Tanker TrailerHigh explosion risk; requires careful driving through traffic.Alloy/MetalFlatbed or BensonModerate speed; visible cargo can attract "hijackers" (NPC or Players).Processors/TVsMule or Pony (Vans)Faster, but fragile. Damage to the vehicle reduces the "Market Value" of the goods.3. The "Global Market" HubInstead of a static menu, implement a physical Commodity Exchange at the Maze Bank Tower or the Terminal Port.Price Fluctuations: Prices for "Consumer Electronics" could rise if players haven't delivered any for a while, simulating scarcity.Export Missions: Once you manufacture 1000 crates of TVs, you must lead a multi-truck convoy to the Port of Los Santos or LSIA for a "Global Export" payout.4. Resource Processing MechanicsTo mimic the SimCity flow, you’ll need a "Warehouse Management" UI:Storage Limits: Factories should have limited input/output storage. If your Smelting Factory is full of Metal but your Computer Assembly line is stalled, you have to manage the "bottleneck."Upgrades: Allow players to spend "Profit" to add a second Alloy Furnace to a factory, increasing the internal processing speed but requiring more frequent raw material deliveries.5. Risk & Gameplay Loop (The "GTA" Twist)Since this is GTA, the industry shouldn't just be a spreadsheet; it should be dangerous.NPC Hijackers: If you are transporting high-value Processors, spawn "Lost MC" or "Merryweather" NPCs to attempt a heist on your convoy.Maintenance Missions: Factories occasionally "break down." You must transport a specific "Tool Kit" item from a hardware store to the factory to resume production.Environmental Hazards: Oil spills or chemical leaks at the Refinery could trigger a 1-star or 2-star wanted level, forcing you to bribe the police or clear the area.Implementation Tip: The "Recycling" ShortcutTo keep the gameplay loop from becoming a "one-way trip" to the north of the map, make the Recycling Center very powerful. Players can collect "Recyclables" (by visiting various dumpsters or scrap heaps in the city) and turn them directly into Metal, Alloy, or Plastic. This gives city-based players a way to enter the manufacturing chain without driving to the desert mines.
