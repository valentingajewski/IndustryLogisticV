Reminder:
Its purpose is to classify how important a site is in the future career mode, so you can later decide things like:

when the player can unlock it
how expensive it should be
how much map influence it gives
what features it unlocks
whether it is a starter foothold, a district hub, or a late-game prestige asset
The values I used were these:

Starter: first accessible assets. Cheap or free. Small footprint. Good for early trucks, first routes, basic depot gameplay.
Local: small neighborhood business asset. Low influence. Good for one district corner, one sink yard, or one small service lot.
Regional: meaningful operational foothold. Medium cost. Should unlock better routing, local bonuses, or more fleet presence.
Core: major strategic asset in a district. High cost. Strong influence. Usually a depot, anchor industry, or major sink that defines control of the area.
Expansion: optional later-game site used to widen the company’s footprint, often for side chains, construction goods, overflow storage, or specialization.
A simple way to think about it is:

Starter = “I can begin here.”
Local = “I operate here.”
Regional = “I control this part of the map.”
Core = “This district is mine.”
Expansion = “This grows my empire sideways.”
