1. Core Logic & Mechanics
- Permit Requirement: Implement a "Contractor Permit" mechanic. When active, players must purchase a specific permit for an industry before they are allowed to transport resources form or to it.
- Data Sourcing: Permit prices for each industry should be dynamically pulled from the LSOL.ini file under the IndustryLicencePrice key.

2. UI Integration (F8 Menu)
Within the Industry sub-menu of the F8 Key interface, integrate the following two buttons:
- Contractor Permit:
    - Function: Displays a list of all industries available in the mod alongside their respective permit prices.
    - Interaction: Clicking an industry entry triggers a confirmation modal (styled after the F8 menu theme).
    - Confirmation Modal: Must explicitly ask the player if they wish to purchase the permit.
    - Actions: Provide "Yes" (execute purchase and unlock transport) and "No" (close modal) buttons.
- Industry Statistics:
    - Function: Opens a dedicated window displaying real-time data and metrics for the currently selected or active industry. Use the current Industry stats

3. Configuration & Difficulty Settings
- Toggle Functionality: Add the Licensing System as a toggleable Difficulty Option.
- Active State: When enabled, all permit requirements and UI elements (buttons/menus) are fully functional.
- Inactive State: When disabled, the "Contractor Permit" button is hidden from the UI, and all resource transport restrictions are bypassed.
